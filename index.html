The listening player sits under the document, behind the 🎧 handle in its bottom left corner: click the handle to open or shut it. It opens by itself when the document already has a track. Use Add tracks… in its chooser, or drop audio files (mp3, m4a or wav) on the window, to attach tracks to the open document; the chooser on the left switches between them and drag reorders them. Drag across the waveform to pick a phrase, then press Space to play just that; drag inside the selection to slide it, its edges to trim it, or click to set where playback starts. Tracks are kept in this browser only, never written into what you export, and are taken off a file in Settings → Storage.

Every section of the side panel rolls up when you click its title, and the Sections button above it chooses which ones are shown at all. Both are remembered for next time.

Split view, at the end of the floating toolbar, opens a second view of the same combined document. Each view has its own file bar, scroll and zoom. Click or focus a view to use the shared toolbar there; its file menu jumps to a file within the combined document. File additions, removals, ordering, annotations and Undo are shared. Drag the divider to resize, use its arrow keys, or double-click to reset. Toggle Split view again to keep the active view in one pane.

Annotations are kept in this browser automatically. File → Export writes every page of the document as one file, as a PDF or as an image, with the annotations drawn in so other apps show them. Pages are laid out as A4 sheets, on screen and in the export: a page bigger than A4 is scaled down to fit, a smaller one is centred at its own size. Asked to include the original files, it keeps them inside, and opening that file in 4em gives back the document with its annotations editable again.

With an AI profile chosen under Settings → Audio transcription, new listening tracks are transcribed in the background. Click a number below the waveform to select its sentence; Space or Play starts it, and clicking the same number again deselects it. The teal timing range and blue free selection both move by dragging inside and resize from their edges. Hold Alt to reach a covered range or Shift while dragging to draw a new free selection. Timing edits apply when released; Undo timing or Ctrl+Z restores the previous timing. Results and corrections are kept in this browser with the audio.
